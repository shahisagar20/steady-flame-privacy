Privacy Policy

Effective June 28, 2026 · Last updated July 26, 2026

Steady Flame is designed to be private by default. This page explains what that means.

The short version

Steady Flame does not collect your data. Everything you create in the app — your reflections, your intentions, your progress, and your settings — stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Steady Flame has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your information. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. The app works fully offline (try it in Airplane Mode). The one exception is the optional Mentor (below): if you turn it on and use it, what you type is sent to Anthropic to generate a reply — and that’s all.

Where your information lives

Your reflections, daily progress, chosen quality, framing preference, and reminder time are stored only in the app’s local database on your device. If you delete the app, that data is removed with it. We never receive a copy.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a single local notification on your device at the time you choose. This happens entirely on your device; no reminder information is sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Children

Steady Flame is rated 9+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Steady Flame contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The optional Mentor sends chat context to Anthropic (see above) only while you are using it.

The Mentor (optional online feature)

Steady Flame 2.0 adds an optional AI Mentor — a short, calm chat. It is off by default and only works if you turn it on in Settings and add your own Anthropic API key.

When you send the Mentor a message, the app sends the following directly from your device to Anthropic (the AI provider) to generate the reply: the message you typed, the running conversation, your chosen quality and today’s lesson, your secular-or-spiritual framing setting, and your most recent evening reflections (with their dates and mood markers). This is used solely to generate the reply — never to track you, never for advertising.

Steady Flame has no server: we store nothing, anywhere. The conversation isn’t even saved on your device — it ends when you close the chat. Your API key is stored only in the iOS Keychain. Anthropic processes your messages under its own terms and privacy policy. Turn the Mentor off (or remove the key) at any time in Settings — the app then makes no network requests at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this page.

Support

Steady Flame works entirely offline and needs no account, so there is nothing to sign in to and no password to recover. If a day will not open, a reminder does not arrive, or anything else is not behaving, email shahisagar20@gmail.com and describe what you saw — a screenshot helps. Replies usually come within a few days.

Two things that are asked often. Missing a day costs you nothing — the app has no streaks; open it again and begin where you are. The Mentor is optional and off by default; it needs your own Anthropic API key, and the rest of the app works fully without it.

Your practice is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and we have no copy to restore, so treat anything you want to keep as worth writing down elsewhere.
